About Us

A Community Concert Series

Absolute Music brings world-class live performance to our community — intimate concerts in the warm, welcoming spaces of Congregation Beth Ami in Santa Rosa.

From the smoky swing of 1930s Paris jazz to the exuberant brass of a full big band, each concert is a chance to experience extraordinary music up close.
